Forge & Fantasy Weapon SFX Pack - By Case Portman Audio

The sounds in this pack are named based on how they were originally designed, but that doesn't mean you have to use them that way!
Feel free to mix and match sounds, layer them together, randomize their pitch and/or volume, add your own effects, or use them in ways I haven't even thought of.

Get creative, experiment, and most importantly - have fun with them!
